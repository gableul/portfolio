# Reconstructing Google Solar from scratch in 36h

*Draft — full write-up coming soon.*

At the BIG Berlin Hack we rebuilt solar-potential estimation for any address in
36 hours. From a single address, **Iconic** generates a 3D model of a house with
optimal placement of solar panels, a heat pump, a battery and an EV charger.

## The pipeline

- A from-scratch reconstruction of Google Solar via a 21-variant algorithm
- Satellite captures (Google + Cesium)
- GPT Image for building isolation
- fal.ai for 3D mesh generation

This won the Reonic track and placed 2nd out of 380+ teams. A detailed technical
breakdown of the 21-variant algorithm and the mesh pipeline is in progress.
