# A proactive voice agent for medical follow-up

*Draft — full write-up coming soon.*

Built at the Alan × Mistral AI hackathon, **Maude** is a voice AI agent
integrated into the Alan app that proactively calls members after a consultation
for personalized medical follow-up.

## Orchestration

- Multilingual (FR / EN)
- Mistral (Voxtral + Small 4) for understanding and generation
- ElevenLabs for natural speech
- LiveKit for real-time, low-latency streaming
- Powered by Thryve wearable data

It won the ElevenLabs track. A deeper dive into the latency budget and the
GDPR-aware data flow is on the way.
