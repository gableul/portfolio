# Optimizing SAFe PI Planning with NSGA-II

*Draft — full write-up coming soon.*

Turning a room full of sticky notes into a six-objective optimization problem
with a human in the loop. This is the accessible companion to my master's thesis,
**SAFESCALE**.

## The idea

- PI Planning is an NP-hard combinatorial problem — an instance of the
  multi-skill RCPSP
- A multi-objective **NSGA-II** optimizer extended to six objectives, including a
  WSJF-weighted tardiness
- A five-step orchestrated multi-agent architecture with conditional
  Human-In-The-Loop
- A deterministic risk model inspired by Goldratt's Critical Chain and Kingman's
  law
